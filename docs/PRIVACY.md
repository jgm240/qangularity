# qAngularity Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 10 October 2026*

**Short version: qAngularity does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.**

## What stays on your device
Settings, saved keys and files, and the pairing with your Flipper are stored locally on your phone or computer. The authors of qAngularity have no access to them and there are no user accounts.

## Network requests the app makes
- **GitHub** (`raw.githubusercontent.com`, `github.com`, `api.github.com`): to look for Angularity FW and app updates and to download update files. GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information, as with any website. See GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Flipper Devices Inc servers** (for example `catalog.flipperzero.one`, `infrared.flipperzero.one`, `update.flipperzero.one`, `flpr.app`): only when you use the matching optional feature (app catalog, infrared database, region settings, key sharing). Those requests are subject to Flipper Devices Inc's own privacy policy, and qAngularity does not control them.

## Bluetooth and permissions
The app talks to your Flipper over Bluetooth Low Energy. Depending on your Android or iOS version it may ask for Bluetooth / Nearby devices, location (needed by older Android versions to scan for Bluetooth devices, not used to track you), notifications, and file access for importing and exporting keys. Each can be denied or revoked in system settings, with the related feature then unavailable.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

## Changes
Changes to this policy will be published in the project repository.
